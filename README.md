# 📊 Data Professional Survey Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyzes responses from a **Data Professional Survey**. It explores who works in data, what they earn, which programming languages they prefer, where they live, and how happy they are in their roles.

![Dashboard Preview](images/PowerBiDashboard.png)

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

To turn raw survey responses into clear, interactive visuals that answer questions such as:

- Which job titles earn the highest average salary?
- What is the favorite programming language for each data role?
- Where do survey respondents live?
- How satisfied are data professionals with their salary and work/life balance?
- How difficult is it to break into the data field?

---

## 🧩 Dashboard Contents

| Visual | Type | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| Total Respondents | Card | Count of unique survey responses |
| Current Age | Card | Respondent age summary |
| Average Salary by Job Title | Bar chart | Average salary for each job title |
| Favorite Programming Language | Column chart | Preferred language by job title |
| Country of Survey Takers | Treemap | Distribution of respondents by country |
| Happiness with Work/Life Balance | Gauge | Average satisfaction score |
| Happiness with Salary | Gauge | Average satisfaction score |
| Difficulty to Break into Data | Donut chart | How hard respondents found entering the field |

## ▶️ How to Open This Project

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Install [Power BI Desktop](https://powerbi.microsoft.com/desktop/) (free, Windows only).
3. Open `DataProfessionalSurveyProject.pbix`.
4. Use the slicers and click on visuals to filter the report interactively.

## 📌 Key Insights

<!-- Replace with your own findings after reviewing the dashboard, e.g.: -->
- _Which job title has the highest average salary?_ - Data Scientist has the highest average salary.
- _Which programming language is most popular overall?_ - Python is most popular overall.
- _Which country has the most respondents?_ - United States has the most respondents.
- _How happy are respondents with salary vs. work/life balance?_ - Respondents are happier with work/life balance (5.7 out of 10) than with salary (4.2 out of 10), a gap of 1.5 points. This suggests data professionals value their flexibility but feel their pay could be higher.
